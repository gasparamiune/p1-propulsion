**The basis is the candidate gnc.json v3.2,** `01-systems-gnc/staging_v3.1/rev4b/v32/gnc_v3.2_rev4b.json` (43d58c4aff164b86). It folds the lead decisions of CHANGELOG lines 204-227 and the REV-GNC-6 corrections. The lead has not published it yet. The ENG-11 FE-plant re-run is staged separately in `rev4b/eng11_fe/` and is not folded into the v3.2 text.

**What the tags mean:**

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SIM | flown in the GNC 6-DOF Monte Carlo |
| DERIVED | computed from SIM tables or interface data |
| ASSUMED | an input without a measurement |
| DATASHEET | maker data |
| TO-VERIFY | needed from the named owner |
| REQ | a lead or founder decision, with the evidence it rests on |

**There is no hardware data.** Matching numbers and passing checks are not physical approval, and nothing in this table is physical approval.
