### 7.3 Sequence (v2.4, replaces the v2.3 sequence)

v2.4 changes, in response to RC-01 and RC-02:
- The torch is a hard gate: no N2O reaches the main chamber before the torch flame is confirmed.
- Pc confirmation gates the pintle ramp.
- The torch valves close on the Pc-confirmation event, not on a clock. N2O closes first.
- Every abort de-energises the NC valve at once and commands the pintle closed in parallel (LEAD 2026-10-13).
- There is no automatic in-flight retry (LEAD 2026-10-13).
- The abort deadline W replaces the fixed 0.35 s check and is selected by the state of the valve-to-seat segment.

The v2.3 sequence is kept in §13 as superseded evidence.

**Epochs** (published in combustion.json `igniter.sequence_v2p4.epochs`):

| Epoch | Event |
|---|---|
| E0 | ECU receives START or RELIGHT. SOL1 (torch N2O) and SOL2 (propane) open; spark at E0 + 0.02 s |
| E1 | Torch flame confirmed: torch-cavity pressure > 2.0 bar abs for 10 ms (lit about 3 bar, unlit about 1.1 bar), or a photodiode; Electronics selects. At E1 the NC valve open command (if closed) and the pintle command to the ignition lift are issued together |
| E2 | Pc confirmed: Pc > 3.0 bar abs for 10 ms (sensor latency 2.3 ms). At E2, SOL1 is de-energised, SOL2 within 20 ms, spark off, and the ramp is released |
| E3 | Thrust >= 90 % of the start or relight command (used for ENG-04 latency, §7.7) |

**ARM preconditions** (v2.4; the rev-2 items are RC-13 b and c):
- Tank at 15-25 C (ENG-01, ENG-12).
- Tank heater hard-disabled at ARM (ENG-12).
- Pintle seated and encoder healthy. NC valve, SOL0, SOL1 and SOL2 closed. SOL0 is the torch isolation valve proposed in §7.5.
- Continuity of the spark primary and of every solenoid.
- Sensors healthy:
  - Pc reads ambient +/- 0.1 bar;
  - Pc transducer range >= 70 bar (ENG-09; RC-13 d);
  - P_feed and P_tank healthy;
  - torch-cavity pressure or photodiode healthy;
  - thermocouples on SOL1, the torch check valve and the injector manifold.
- Chamber vented: Pc within 0.1 bar of ambient.
- Ground only:
  - stand vertical, nozzle down;
  - burst disc fitted (§7.6);
  - N2 purge connected;
  - remote operation, with the crew outside the exclusion distance (§9.2).

**Start and relight sequence.** The same sequence is used for a pad start, a ground restart and an in-flight relight.

1. **Before E0** (relight only): settling confirmed by the acceleration history (§2.4) and chamber vented. The NC valve stays closed through the coast (LEAD rule).
2. **E0:** SOL1 and SOL2 open; spark train 50 Hz, 50 mJ, from E0 + 0.02 s. The pintle stays seated and the NC valve stays closed.
3. **Torch deadline:** if E1 has not occurred by E0 + 0.25 s, the torch no-light row of the abort matrix applies.
   - Under the ASSUMED delay distributions, E0 -> E1 has p99 0.138 s and maximum about 0.15 s (§7.7).
   - No main-chamber N2O has been admitted at that point.
4. **E1:** the NC valve open command (if closed) and the pintle command to the ignition lift are issued together.
   - The ignition lift is 12.5 % flow: 0.206 mm at 20 C, 0.216 mm at 15 C, 0.198 mm at 25 C, 0.219 mm at the A1 relight state. It is reached 45-46 ms after the command (SIM).
   - Opening the valve against an open pintle vents any gas in the segment through the injector. A dead-ended segment is never compressed, so the v2.3 bleed-lift priming step (H2) is no longer needed.
   - The lift is held until E2 (ENG-04 ignition-lift hold).
5. **Abort deadline W,** counted from E1. If E2 has not occurred by E1 + W, the main no-light row applies. W depends on the segment state:

   | Segment state | Condition | W |
   |---|---|---|
   | Proven liquid-full | Ground restart after a liquid prime, with the injector manifold thermocouple at least 1 K below T_sat(P_tank) | 0.40 s |
   | All other cases | Pad start on a fresh or gas-filled line; every in-flight relight | 0.85 s |

   Under the ASSUMED delays (§7.7), E1 -> E2 has these maxima over 200 000 samples:
   - 0.357 s with a liquid segment;
   - 0.815 s with a full-vapour segment and the valve closed.

   No sample is a false abort at either W. The inventory and pressure consequences of W are in §7.6.
6. **E2:**
   - SOL1 (N2O) is de-energised first and SOL2 (propane) within 20 ms, so the torch ends propane-only and never runs N2O-only.
   - At E2, Pc is about 3 bar, below the propane supply (6.4 bar at 10 C), so propane still flows until it is cut.
   - The spark stops.
   - The pintle ramps to the command at <= 2500 N/s. The drive must still deliver >= 2 F_max/s (ENG-03).
7. **Run:** Pc loop at 1 kHz (Electronics). The abort monitors are listed in §7.4.

**Normal shutdown** (unchanged order, plus a time-out; LEAD 2026-10-13):
1. Pre-MECO throttle-down to <= 75 % (LEAD; ENG-05).
2. The pintle closes at full drive speed.
3. The NC valve is de-energised when the seat is confirmed (encoder at the seat and Pc < 2 bar abs), or 0.30 s after the shutdown command, whichever comes first.
   - The 0.30 s time-out is DERIVED: full stroke at 16.7 mm/s is 0.24 s, plus 10 ms lag and 6.5 ms command latency.
4. Trapped liquid in the valve-to-seat segment relieves to the tank side through the differential check valve S1 (§2.7).
5. Ground only: after a 30 s hold, the post-test safing procedure applies (§10.2).

**Abort** (any row of §7.4):
- the NC valve is de-energised immediately;
- the pintle is commanded closed in parallel;
- SOL0, SOL1 and SOL2 are de-energised and the spark stops;
- if the pintle shows no closing progress 0.10 s after the command, servo power is released and the pintle spring closes the unpowered servo (Electronics pintle_rail_v3; B-PNT-1 back-drive check);
- the ECU reports the abort to GNC (drogue per the GNC policy).

**No automatic retry** (LEAD 2026-10-13):
- In flight, a no-light means the NC valve is de-energised and the pintle closed at once; the vehicle is accepted as lost under the drogue.
- On the ground, the stand is safed (§10.2) and the cause is investigated before any new attempt.

Research proposal for the lead, not adopted (in-flight torch-only retry):
- **Proposal.** If E1 fails (torch no-light), no main-chamber N2O has been admitted, so a single torch retry adds no hard-start inventory.
- **Hypothesis.** The retry raises P(relight) without raising hard-start exposure.
- **Comparator.** The adopted no-retry rule.
- **Evidence needed.**
  - The torch no-light probability from IGN-1;
  - GNC's landing margin for a relight about 0.25-0.4 s later. A1 relights at 36.5-37.3 m, descending at 21.3-21.6 m/s.
- **Refutation criterion.** The GNC margin is negative, or the IGN-1 torch reliability makes the retry irrelevant.

**Model transients** (SIM, ASSUMED delays; replaces the v2.3 paragraph):
- The start and relight transients of the adopted sequence are in §7.7.
- The v2.3 figures were 0.28-0.29 s to 90 %. They assumed a 0.10 s torch-to-main delay, a direct step to the command, no lift hold and no valve step. They are superseded.

### 7.5 Torch N2O line and flashback (RC-01 points 1, 2 and 4; C4)

The v2.3 contradiction (C4) is removed:
- v2.3 closed propane at 0.4 s and torch N2O at 0.6 s, which gave an N2O-only interval through a hot torch.
- v2.4 closes SOL1 (N2O) first at E2 and SOL2 (propane) within 20 ms. The torch ends propane-only. No N2O sweep is kept.

Requirements for the torch feed (for Mechanical and Electronics, through the lead):

| ID | Requirement | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| R-T1 | No N2O-only flow through a hot torch, in any sequence or abort. SOL1 closes no later than SOL2. If IGN-1 shows a propane-rich tail damages the torch, "both together" is the alternative | RC-01 point 1; ENG-05 |
| R-T2 | Dead-ended N2O volume between the last N2O closure and the torch orifice <= 0.05 cm3. SOL1 sits on a thermal stand-off at the torch inlet, with its body <= T_lim (ASSUMED 120 C, TO-SET at IGN-1) through the burn and the soak | RC-01 point 4 (hot dead-ended line). | R-T3 | Two closures in series between the tank and the torch. Baseline: torch N2O tapped on the tank side of the NC valve, so the torch lights before the main valve moves; SOL0 (NC) at the tap (fixed side, cold) and SOL1 (NC) at the torch. Alternative, if Mechanical keeps both torch solenoids on the fixed side:
- Tap the torch N2O downstream of the NC valve, so the NC valve is the second closure.
- Fit the check valve on a stand-off whose temperature is shown <= T_lim by IGN-1/T1 thermocouples.
- Cost: the dead-ended line from the fixed-side solenoid to the check valve stays. Its acceptance rests on the measured check-valve temperature.
- Cost: on a pad start from a fresh line, the torch has no N2O until the segment is primed, so the torch-first gate needs the valve to open first. That re-introduces the dead-ended compression on priming (H2), unless the v2.3 bleed-lift step is kept.

- Pad start from a cold chamber: the only fuel present before main ignition is the torch propane. That is 0.027 g over 0.30 s of torch operation, which can burn 0.27 g of N2O at stoichiometry (O/F_st 9.98, DERIVED). This is 14-22 % of the fast part, so the fuel-assisted row does not apply. Grain pyrolysis before main ignition is not credited (unknown).
- Relight into a hot chamber: pyrolysis vapour from the hot grain can premix with arriving N2O. The fuel-assisted row applies if ignition is delayed and then sudden.
  - With tau_fast >= 5 ms, the peak stays below MEOP.
  - With tau_fast about 1 ms, it reaches 48-76 bar: above MEOP, and above proof at the long-residence end.
  - The torch-first gate is the control: the flame is present before any N2O arrives.

**Chosen lift and deadline, and the written bound** (RC-02 closure statement):
- **Lift.** The ignition lift is 12.5 %. A 20 % lift would raise the fast part to 1.42 g and the 1 ms fuel-assisted peak to 55.5 bar.
- **Deadline.** W is 0.40 s for a proven liquid segment and 0.85 s otherwise, counted from E1.
- **Pad start, T1-T3 and A1 launch:**
  - credible peak <= 37.7 bar (20 C) and <= 39.7 bar (A1 state);
  - margin >= 5.3 bar to MEOP and >= 27.8 bar to proof.
- **Relight, T4 and flight:**
  - the same decomposition bound applies;
  - the fuel-assisted bound exceeds MEOP if the fast part reacts in <= about 3 ms;
  - in flight the vehicle is already lost on a no-light (LEAD), and the hazard on a delayed ignition is to the vehicle;
  - on the ground (T4) it is covered by the stand provisions below.
- **Inputs.** Every input of the credible case is ASSUMED. IGN-1 and T1 supply the ignition delay; nothing measures the pooled-liquid reaction time.

**Stand provisions** (IGN-1 with main N2O, and T1-T4):
- **Orientation.** Vertical, nozzle down, so liquid drains through the throat instead of pooling below a horizontal lip (RC-02 point 5).
- **Test article.** Proof-tested chamber (67.5 bar), remote operation, crew beyond the exclusion distance (§9.2).
- **Burst disc.** Stand-side burst disc or blow-out, 55 bar nominal (between MEOP 45 and proof 67.5), >= 16 mm and preferably 20 mm. It caps slow events (tau >= 5-10 ms) near 55 bar (`burst_disc_effect_bar`):
  - for 12 g at tau 10 ms: 79 bar without a disc, 55 bar with 16 mm;
  - for 12 g at tau 5 ms: 69.7 bar with 16 mm, 54.9 bar with 20 mm;
  - it does not help at tau 2 ms.

  The disc is test-article only; Mechanical places it and protects it from hot gas.
- **Wait after a no-light.** After the deadline W the chamber holds 6.5-7.2 g of liquid at W 0.40 and 13.7-15.2 g at W 0.85, with no drainage (horizontal bound).
  - Wall conduction alone needs >= 4.9-6.1 s (W 0.40) and >= 21.8-26.9 s (W 0.85) even with the film spread over the whole port. A pool takes far longer.
  - An N2 purge at 10 g/s needs 2.2-5.1 s if ideal and 10.9-25.4 s at 20 % effectiveness (ASSUMED).
  - The v2.3 rule "wait >= 5 s" is therefore not supported and is withdrawn.
  - Ground rule: N2 purge >= 60 s at >= 10 g/s (>= 2.4x the 20 % effectiveness figure), then a hold of >= 15 min before approach with Pc at ambient and the head thermocouples falling, then the safing procedure (§10.2).
- **Coast leak ceiling** (RC-13 g), recomputed. Accumulated seat leak over the longest A1 coast must stay <= 10 % of the smallest credible fast part:
  - 0.10 x 1.22 g over 22.21 s gives a ceiling of 0.0055 g/s (DERIVED);
  - this replaces 0.039 g/s, which inherited the 8.74 g inventory;
  - the proposed CF-2 acceptance (<= 1 scc/min N2) is 2.1e-5 g/s of N2 (DERIVED), about 260 times below the ceiling in mass. Converting a gas leak at the CF-2 test pressure into a liquid-N2O leak at tank pressure depends on the leak path and is TO-VERIFY with N2O at CF-3.

### 7.7 Relight latency of the adopted sequence (RC-03)

Method (`start_latency_v2p4.py`, `relight_latency_sup_v2p4.py`):
- Part 1: grain_q1d runs of the ignition-lift start at each state and lift, over a x0.6-1.5, n 0.35-0.68, preheat 0.15-0.6 and eta_c* 0.85/0.92 (ramp 2500 N/s, feed-forward and with a Pc loop K 15).
- Part 2: a 200 000-sample Monte Carlo (seed 2024) over ASSUMED delay distributions:
  - solenoid opening U(0.005, 0.020) s;
  - torch light U(0.02, 0.10) s;
  - flame detection U(0.005, 0.030) s;
  - valve flow onset U(0.05, 0.15) s;
  - main ignition delay U(0.05, 0.30) s (the review's band);
  - segment vapour fraction U(0, 1) x a 0.31 s purge (full vapour: 0.31 s).
- No distribution is MEASURED; IGN-1, T1 and T4 replace them.

**Epoch definition for ENG-04** (for the lead to adopt):
- ENG-04's 0.5 s is counted from E0, the RELIGHT command received by the ECU, to E3, 90 % of the relight command.

**What keeps the segment liquid in the coast**, and so keeps relight inside ENG-04 at the median:
- the differential check valve S1 (§2.7), which vents segment vapour to the tank;
- low heat input to the coil and manifold (§2.7: 5 % vapour at 10 W, 16-19 % at 30 W, 38-45 % at 70 W, full at about 200 W);
- the O3b routing condition (§2.7).

The segment state at relight is not measured by any planned sensor. A thermocouple on the injector manifold is the proposed indicator for selecting W. Its ability to see vapour is TO-TEST at T4.

**Options for the lead** (ENG-04 is not met at p95 under the ASSUMED delays):
1. Accept ENG-04 as a median requirement, with GNC planning on the distributions above.
2. Count ENG-04 from the valve command and pre-open the valve; this only helps if the segment holds vapour.
3. Keep the NC valve open in coasts once the CF-2 seat-leak result meets the 0.0055 g/s ceiling. This removes the valve onset but not the vapour.
4. Insulate or re-route the O3b coil and manifold so that heat input stays <= 10 W.
5. Shorten the ignition delay with a hotter torch or a longer torch overlap; IGN-1 measures the effect.
