## 0. Checkpoint

**Claim.**
1. **The paper design point stands.** The independent review reproduced the thermochemistry, sizing, injector model, throat heat flux, floor separation and the grain at n 0.5 to within 0.1-1 % (review §3). No input to that core changed in v2.4. The engine: N2O/ABS hybrid, printed straight-port grain S230, servo-driven pintle behind the normally-closed MV-P, spark-ignited N2O/propane torch, conical graphite-throat nozzle with eps 2.0; 500 N at sea level needs Pc 25.01-25.63 bar over the eta_cf band 0.975-0.95, against the 25 bar cap (§4.3). The throttle floor closes in the models at the 20 % requirement (ENG-02) and at the 12.5 % stretch; 25 % is a flight fallback only (RC-13 a; v2.3 called it the requirement). Three or more starts per flight are built into the sequence, but an in-flight relight after a coast lights reliably only with the fix of Claim 3.
2. **v2.4 revises what the review found unfit for the stated use** (the basis for N2O cold-flow, torch and static-fire tests):
   - sequence, abort matrix and torch feed (RC-01): the v2.8 relight design adopted by the lead, torch-first gate 0.16 s, no N2O-only torch interval, abort matrix A1-A14 (§7.3-§7.5);
   - hard start (RC-02): a three-node relief network bounds it. The credible peak is <= 44.9 bar with a sharp port entrance and <= 41.5 bar with the rounded entrance (r >= 4 mm, now required on every grain), below the 45 bar MEOP and independent of the inventory. Stand: aft DN32 disc, R1 = R2 = 0.35 s, 13.5 g at 24 C against a 13.84 g allowable (§7.6);
   - relight latency (RC-03): published per line state. ENG-04 (<= 0.5 s to 90 %) is met at the median only on a liquid line; no relight after a coast starts on a liquid line (§7.7);
   - relief, safing and stored energy (RC-04, RC-05, RC-12): S1 back-relief, 9.5 MPa disc, MAWP >= 8.42 MPa on the N2O side, tank relief 6.9 MPa (proposed), post-test and post-landing safing, stored-energy table (§2.7, §9, §10);
   - throat life (RC-06): 0.38-0.60 A1 flights per insert on the literature-scaled method and the gnc.json v3 A1 p99 cycle; plan on one insert per flight (§5.4);
   - web band (RC-07): on B1 85 m the corner a x1.5 with n 0.35 burns through at 17.0-17.8 s; the 62 m cap with the lead's gating rule covers it, and GNC has a web-against-time law for the burn limit (§4.6);
   - minor items RC-08 to RC-20: corrected or answered (REVISION-RESPONSE).
3. **A new finding governs the relight.** The 44 cm3 valve-to-seat segment is an inverted U. Every coast fills it with vapour: 26.5-44 cm3 ahead of the first liquid at RELIGHT across Mechanical's six heat cases. At the flight window W 0.65 s, 45-100 % of would-be relights abort. Combustion recommends **option (a), a crest bleed before E0**: P_abort 0 in every heat case at W 0.65 s, at a cost of up to 28.3 g of N2O vented per relight and a new valve. Option (d), early MV opening under the drogue, is refuted for this routing. Widening W was rejected by the lead. No prep-path fallback exists on the 26.6 g inventory basis (§2.8).
4. **REV-GNC-1 items** answered here: RG-03 (oxidiser-rich low-a operation at 62 m: c*, Isp, throat oxidation, stability; §4.6, §5.4, §8), the burn-limit law (§4.6) and RG-20 (coast migration on GNC's time histories; §2.8).

This is a design basis for cold-flow and static-fire testing. It is not a demonstrated engine. A higher-fidelity model is not proof of higher accuracy (AGENTS.md).
