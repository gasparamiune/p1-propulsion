### 2.6 Feed line, hose and sense line (v2.4)

The feed layout in v2.4 follows the lead's decisions of 2026-10-13:
- Main valve: MV-P, a balanced poppet. Cv 3.6 (Cd 0.65 ASSUMED), seat 11 mm, lift 2.75 mm.
  - Closing time: about 30 ms with the dashpot; 0.10 s is the bounding value until bench B-MV-1.
  - Rating: MAWP >= 8.42 MPa, proof >= 10.5 MPa.
- MV-P location: in the FS-01 module at the top of the zone (option A), with a fixed 3/8 in line down the wall to the coil (LEAD).
- Back-relief check: FS-01 also carries a check valve (crack 0.03-0.07 MPa) that relieves the segment back to the tank (§2.7).

Hose and sense line are unchanged from v2.3:
- **Hose.** -6 PTFE-lined braid across the gimbal.
  - Velocity: 6.3 m/s at 0.241 kg/s and 6.5-7.1 m/s at 0.26 kg/s.
  - Friction: 8-17 kPa.
  - Surge on slow closure: 14-106 kPa, for a 40-150 ms closure and a 0.4-0.8 m line.
- **Pc sense line.**
  - 1/8 in tube (1.6 mm ID), no longer than 0.3 m; transducer dead volume <= 0.3 cm3.
  - Natural frequency >= 144 Hz in N2O-like gas and >= 188 Hz in N2.
  - Phase lag <= 0.64 deg at 50 Hz (DERIVED).

**Priming.** The v2.3 bleed-lift rule is no longer a separate step. The v2.8 sequence (§7.3) handles priming on two paths:
- **Primed** (P_feed >= 0.5 P_tank): the MV-P opens at E0 with the pintle seated.
  - Any gas in the segment is already at >= 0.5 P_tank.
  - Isentropic compression of saturated vapour from 0.5 P_tank to P_tank heats it to at most 310 / 315 / 319 K at 15 / 20 / 24 C (CoolProp, DERIVED). From 0.8 P_tank the bound is 295-304 K.
  - This is far below the 614-646 K of a line compressed from 1 bar (Table 2.7). No bleed is needed.
- **Unprimed** (P_feed < 0.5 P_tank, including the first start on a fresh line): Electronics keeps the v2.7 preparation.
  - A 0.50-0.56 s prepress with a 0.06 s bleed lift (Electronics v2.7/v2.8), so a gas-filled dead end is never compressed fast.
  - Electronics confirms that the bleed and the >= 0.3 s opening ramp are present in v2.8 firmware (open item, §12).

### 2.8 The valve-to-seat segment at relight (44 cm3) and the retained-vapour requirement (v2.4)

Basis files:
- `segment_recheck_v2p4.json`, `relight_window_rule_seg44_v2p4.json` and `relight_latency_sup_seg44_v2p4.json`;
- `segment_drain_v2p4.json` and `post_abort_pocket_v2p4.json`.

Common basis:
- Purge times use the SIM C7 basis of 0.31 s per 21.42 cm3 at the 12.5 % lift of the A1 state (0.2192 mm). They are scaled with volume, and by 0.2192/0.2039 = 1.074 for the v2.8 default lift of 0.2039 mm (DERIVED).
- Delay distributions are those of §7.7 (ASSUMED).

**Segment contents** (DERIVED, 44 cm3):

| State | Liquid-full [g] | Vapour-full [g] | Whole-segment purge [s] |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 relight state (286.7 K, 43.5 bar) | 36.5 | 5.64 | 0.637 at 0.2192 mm; 0.685 at 0.2039 mm |

**Coast boil-off with S1 working.** The segment is held at P_tank + 0.07 MPa, i.e. 0.68 K of subcooling at the A1 state.
- Boiling the whole segment off needs about 7.05 kJ: 392 / 122 / 35 s at 18 / 58 / 200 W.
- Over the A1 coast (19.1-22.2 s) the vapour generated is 1.4-1.7 g at 18 W and 5.4-6.4 g at 58 W.
- With 1 g settling, the vapour leaves through S1 at the top (FS-01 is at the high point) or collects at the hot end.
- Filling the whole 44 cm3 with retained vapour needs 1092 J at the A1 state (rho_l 830.2, rho_v 128.2 kg/m3, h_fg 193.5 kJ/kg). That takes 18.8 s at 58 W and 60.7 s at 18 W.

- With no abort in the 200 000 samples, the largest pocket is 18.9 cm3 at 0.2039 mm (20.3 cm3 at 0.2192 mm).
- **Flight requirement** (LEAD 2026-10-13): retained vapour <= 19 cm3 at RELIGHT. At 19 cm3, P_abort is 5e-4.
- Under the same model, a mean segment heat input of <= 24 W meets the requirement over a 22.2 s coast even if all the vapour is retained.
- **Settling.** The A1 settling history (§2.4) has 6.66 s of negative axial force (liquid forward, minimum -1.24 m/s2), then 13.93 s settled at about 1 g before relight. Vapour generated in the coast therefore rises to the high point or vents through S1 before relight. That supports the requirement, but it does not show it.
- **Closure.** The check closes when two inputs arrive (open items, §12):
  - GNC: the A1 axial acceleration from MECO to RELIGHT;
  - Mechanical: the segment heat input on the zone-top route, including the wall line.
- If the requirement cannot be met, insulation or a re-route is required (LEAD).

**What option (c) does not see.** A segment full of saturated vapour reads P_feed = P_tank, so it reads PRIMED.
- Electronics' UNPRIMED flag (P_feed < 0.5 P_tank) therefore does not detect it.
- The retained-vapour requirement, not the flag, is the control for flight.
- Mechanical's seat-leak drain analysis (CHANGELOG 2026-10-13) shows that unpriming the segment within a coast needs >= 1 g/s, i.e. a stuck-open pintle. UNPRIMED in flight is therefore a fault indication.

- **Normal shutdown is pintle-first** (LEAD 2026-10-13). The MV is de-energised on seat confirmation or after a 0.30 s timeout. The segment stays primed for the next relight.
- **An abort is MV-first.** The NC valve is the fail-safe and closes de-energised at once, and the MV-first order empties about half the segment.
- **After any abort, the next attempt is treated as a vapour-segment start** (LEAD). The post-abort pocket matters for that next attempt:
  - Without compression, the pocket of 22.2-24.3 cm3 gives P_abort 0.18-0.31 at W 0.65 s.
  - Compressed to tank pressure before the lift, it is 18.4-20.1 cm3 (isothermal, no condensation credit, ASSUMED) and gives P_abort 0-0.05.
  - The MV-P opening at E0 starts that compression 0.03-0.15 s before the lift. The unprimed-path prepress (0.50-0.56 s) completes it.
- **Combustion recommendation** (for the lead; Electronics implements): after any abort, the ECU latches UNPRIMED until a pintle-first shutdown has re-primed the segment. The FC then plans 1.41 s and the prepress runs.
  - This is not a wrong UNPRIMED in GNC's sense, because the segment does hold vapour.
  - Electronics and GNC confirm (open item, §12).
- **In 1 g on the stand,** a pocket at the top transits the segment and reaches the pintle after the coil liquid. That gives a Pc dip during the ramp. It is a T4 measurement item, not a model result.

The stand rules R1-R4 at 44 cm3:
- **R1 and R2** (first start 0.40 s; every relight 0.50 s) are unchanged. Liquid-line inventories do not depend on volume, and with the stand vertical the hot-end trap of about 8 cm3 governs the vapour.
- **R3** (0.65 s when superheat is proven at both MSS-1 and MSS-2):
