**Question (LEAD 2026-10-13).** What governs ignition is the vapour between the first liquid and the pintle, in flow order, at RELIGHT. How much is there (LO and HI heat), does W 0.65 s light it, and if not, which fix?

**Answer.**
- The segment is an inverted U. Vapour that forms between the U-bottom and the seat cannot reach the MV or S1 by buoyancy, in either coast phase. Mechanical's reading that the head end cannot vent is confirmed, and it applies to the whole U (27.87 cm3), not only to the head end.
- Vapour ahead of the first sustained liquid at RELIGHT, over GNC's MECO-to-RELIGHT windows of 17.8-21.8 s:
  - LO heat: 26.5-29.3 cm3 effective. The U is 95-100 % vapour.
  - HI heat: 38.7 cm3 effective (the U full, plus the void of the overflow rising through the wall line).
- W 0.65 s does not light it. The probability that a relight which would light is aborted instead is 0.45-0.62 (LO) and 1.0 (HI).
- The 19 cm3 requirement is not met by the zone-top routing. No passive coast condition meets it, because the LO case already fills 95 % of the U.
- **Recommendation: (b), plan every relight as a vapour start with W 1.05 s.** It lights every state of this analysis (P_abort 0 in 200 000 samples). It costs GNC's planning time (1.21 s primed, 1.77 s unprimed) and raises the unignited liquid-line inventory to 36.8-40.3 g against the 26.6 g accepted. Both need the lead (and GNC's P cost).
- **Fallback: (a)**, a crest prime bleed, if the lead does not accept the inventory or GNC's cost is too high.
- **(d) does nothing** for the U.
- **The lasting fix is Mechanical's:** a U of 18.9 cm3 or less, or no U. That restores W 0.65 s.

**Why the U traps vapour.**
- With f_z > 0 (descent), vapour rises toward +z. The crest (z 204.6) is the top of the U. The only way from the crest to the MV and S1 runs down the riser and coil to the U-bottom (z 45) and then up the wall line. Buoyancy cannot carry vapour down 160 mm, so it collects at the crest and fills the U from the top down.
  - Liquid displaced from the U leaves through the U-bottom, up the wall line, and out through S1 to the tank.
  - When the vapour front reaches the U-bottom, further vapour overflows into the wall line and vents through S1. The U then holds as much vapour as it can.
- With f_z < 0 (coast-up), vapour drifts toward -z. The U-bottom is then the top, and both legs leave it toward +z, so nothing leaves the U either.
- The dead-end bore leg (4.51 cm3) can only lose liquid by boiling. In the LO case it keeps a plug of up to 2.9 cm3 at the seat if the crest held liquid at the descent onset; in the HI case it boils dry.
- Lateral specific force (GNC: up to 3.1 m/s2 at the seat; angle up to 71 deg from the axis while f_z > 0.1 g; rates up to 194 deg/s near the drogue turnover) is brief. Emptying the U would need the U-bottom held above the crest. With the coil end about 54 mm off the axis, that is a tilt beyond about 71 deg held for the >= 4 s that vapour needs to travel about 0.4 m at <= 0.1 m/s. Nothing in the history does that (DERIVED).

**Filter mesh.** A fully wetted 15 um mesh has a bubble point of about 700 Pa (4 sigma/d, ASSUMED pore = rating). The buoyancy head is 172 Pa across the upper trap and at most 658 Pa across the lower trap, so in the coast the mesh holds vapour below it. That changes where the vapour sits inside the U, not how much there is. In flow the mesh passes gas at any pressure drop above 700 Pa, which is negligible against the bar-level drops at the lift.

**Vapour generated and the pocket at RELIGHT.** Vapour in the U = heat into the U x v_fg/h_fg (0.0388 cm3/J). All of it is retained: no condensation sink, because the walls are hotter than the liquid. The S1-crack subcooling credit is 30 J, about 1.2 cm3, and is shown but not taken. Heat is scaled from Mechanical's 18.67 s coast to GNC's windows with the FE bore Q(t) shape (linear extrapolation of the last 5 samples, ASSUMED for every element). The pocket ahead of the first sustained liquid is the smaller of the vapour generated and the U volume less the liquid plug kept in the bore leg. A liquid plug at the seat is counted as dead time (V_plug / 43.1 cm3/s at the ignition lift, i.e. 1.49 cm3 of equivalent vapour per cm3 of plug). That is conservative. In HI the wall-line void of the overflow (drift flux) is added.

**Flow-order structure and a second-order risk.** From the pintle back, the LO state at RELIGHT is: [bore-leg plug <= 2.9 cm3 of liquid] [vapour] [liquid from the U-bottom on]. The plug flows for about 67 ms, shorter than the minimum assumed ignition delay plus the 10 ms Pc confirmation, so E2 on the plug alone is not expected under the ASSUMED delays. If a faster ignition did reach E2 on the plug, the vapour behind it would follow during the ramp and could trip the flame-out monitor A3 (§7.4) after E2. The same applies to module vapour if the S1 port is not at the module's high point. Open item (Electronics with Combustion, CF-4 data): A3 arming and persistence in the first 0.4 s after E2.

- A pintle pre-bleed (venting the U through the chamber before E0) was considered and set aside. It needs the same purge time as (b), sends 3.9-5.4 g of unignited vapour through a hot chamber without the torch, and does not shorten the plan.

**Recommendation, with numbers.** Adopt (b) now: P_abort 0 for LO and HI, no hardware, and the hard-start credible peaks of §7.6 do not depend on the inventory (<= 41.5 bar with the rounded entrance, <= 44.9 bar sharp). Two conditions belong to the lead:
1. Accept the unignited inventory of up to 40.3 g (40.0 g at 24 C) on the credible-case basis, as was done for 26.6 g.
2. Accept GNC's P cost of planning 1.21 s (1.77 s unprimed).

If either is refused, adopt (a), subject to Mechanical's port and the valve's failure modes. In both cases Mechanical should study a smaller U.

**Research proposal (not adopted): abort deadline referenced to liquid arrival.**
- Hypothesis: the chamber pressure at the ignition lift separates vapour flow from liquid flow. Cold liquid flow gives 0.15-0.25 of the lit Pc; vapour gives less, about 28 % of the liquid mass flow. Liquid arrival can then be detected within about 20 ms.
- Rule: an abort at (liquid arrival + 0.65 s), capped at 1.05 s from the lift. This would bound the unignited liquid at the W 0.65 s level (<= 25 g) whatever the vapour pocket, and would let the stand light vapour starts within its disc allowable.
- Comparator: (b) with a fixed W of 1.05 s.
- Evidence needed: CF-4 traces of Pc and P_feed at the ignition lift with a heated U.
- Refutation criterion: the vapour and liquid signatures overlap, or detection takes longer than 50 ms.

**What the PRIMED flag does not see.** A segment full of saturated vapour reads P_feed = P_tank, so it reads PRIMED. The UNPRIMED flag (P_feed < 0.5 P_tank) therefore cannot detect coast vapour (REV-GNC-1 RG-01, RG-08). Under (b) every relight is planned as a vapour start, so the flag decides only the unprimed fault path (prepress). Mechanical's seat-leak drain analysis (CHANGELOG 2026-10-13) shows that unpriming the segment within a coast needs >= 1 g/s, i.e. a stuck-open pintle. UNPRIMED in flight is therefore a fault indication.

- **R1 and R2, 0.35 s.** At 24 C the liquid line admits 13.5 g against the 13.84 g allowable of the aft DN32 disc with the rounded entrance, a margin of 0.3 g. With a sharp entrance (9.09 g) the rule would fail by 4.4 g, so the rounded entrance is required on every stand grain (§7.6).
- **Abort rate at 0.35 s** (`relight_vapour_states_v2p4.json`): 0.0051 for a primed (liquid) start. Any vapour ahead of the liquid aborts: 1.0 for a U full of vapour, and 0.43-0.55 even after a crest bleed if the unswept bore leg holds a plug (bounding).
- **Stand relights therefore need the U primed.** The vertical stand at 1 g reproduces the flight trap. Recommended stand procedure (GSE, stand only):
  1. Open the MV with the pintle seated.
  2. Bleed at the T4 / P_manifold crest port to a safe vent until liquid arrives, then close it.
  3. Run the unprimed path's prepress (the 0.10 mm bleed lift vents part of the bore leg through the injector).
  4. Command START or RELIGHT.
  T4 measures the resulting abort rate.
- **Test coverage.** No stand test can light a vapour start within the 13.84 g allowable: a 27.87 cm3 pocket needs W >= about 0.80 s. Cold flow CF-4 (§10) measures the purge, IGN-1 the torch and T4 primed relights. The first lit vapour start would be the first flight relight, unless the lead approves a stand basis for one, e.g. a dedicated article with head-end relief or a test under the credible-case basis. This is open item OI-23 for the lead.
- **Boil-dry on the stand.** After a hot hold of >= about 100 s (58 W) to 330 s (18 W), the whole segment boils dry.
