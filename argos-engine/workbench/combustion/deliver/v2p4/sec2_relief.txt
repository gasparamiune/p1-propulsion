**MEOP and the relief (RC-12, for the lead).**
- The set point is now <= MEOP: 6.9 against 7.0 MPa.
- During relief, the parts can still see the relieving pressure: 7.59 MPa in the heater case (10 % accumulation) and 8.35 MPa in a fire (21 %).
- A part sized to SF 1.5 on yield at 7.0 MPa keeps SF 1.38 at 7.59 MPa and 1.26 at 8.35 MPa.
- Proposal: check the N2O-side parts (tank, NC valve, S1, hose, riser, block, manifold) for no yield at 8.35 MPa and SF >= 1.38 at 7.59 MPa. COTS ratings are checked against 8.35 MPa (Mechanical).
- The alternative is MEOP >= 7.6 MPa with the relief at 6.9 MPa. The lead decides.

**Flight tank against temperature** (DERIVED):
- The 2.00 kg / 3.17 L load becomes liquid-full at 306.6 K (33.5 C) and 6.80 MPa.
- Above that, pressure rises at 0.4-0.5 MPa/K and the relief flows liquid or supercritical fluid.
- The 15-25 C arm window stays below it. A pad hold in the sun with the heater disabled is the credible way to reach it, and the 25 C ARM check at E0 guards it (§3.6).
