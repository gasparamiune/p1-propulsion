## 0. Checkpoint

**Claim.** The v2.3 design point stands: N2O/ABS hybrid, 500 N at Pc 25 bar (software cap), Dt 13.724 mm, eps 2.0, S230 straight-port grain, servo pintle with a normally-closed main valve (MV-P). The reviewer reproduced the thermochemistry, sizing, injector model, throat heat flux, floor separation and grain at n 0.5 to 0.1-1 %; that core is not repeated here. v2.4 adds or replaces the following, each with its evidence:

1. **Sequence and abort logic (RC-01; §7.3-7.5).** The relight follows the firmware v2.8 epochs (E0 T_fire, E1 torch confirmed, E2 main light, E3 steady). E1 needs P_torch AND the photodiode (2oo2) and the torch is supervised from E1 to E2 (FA-1 (a) and (b), REQUIRED of Electronics, LEAD line 164). Light windows W: 0.40 s at a flight first start, 0.65 s at a flight relight, 0.35 s on the stand. A5 is deleted and replaced by the LIGHT_SPIKE record; A4 trips at 30 bar raw for 5 ms; A14-A16 cover the bleed valves, the purge and the torch. MV closed in coasts; normal shutdown pintle-first, abort MV-first.
2. **Hard start (RC-02; §7.6).** Decomposition-only late light: head peaks 43.7 / 43.1 / 42.0 bar at a 15 / 20 / 25 C pad start with the sharp entrance, 40.6 / 40.1 / 39.3 bar rounded; 37.8 bar (36.7 rounded) at the A1 relight (SIM, `late_light_tables_v2p4.json`). MEOP 45 bar holds for that case and the rounded entrance is REQUIRED. The v2.4-draft 44.9 bar is superseded. A **fuel-assisted** fast part at a relight reaches 48.9-75.5 bar at a 1 ms burn time (<= 55.5 bar at 5 ms, <= 41.4 bar at 10 ms; SIM on ASSUMED burn time and fuel fraction): MEOP is NOT shown for it. It is FMEA item FA-1, a single-control hazard accepted by the lead for design continuation only (§9.1).
3. **Relight with retained vapour (RG-01; §2.8).** Design basis (LEAD line 164): the coil re-route plus a pre-scheduled two-tap purge on the decided path (two equal 0.707 mm restrictors, Cd 0.70 ASSUMED, valves non-controlling), 2.5 s of flow ending at E0 - 0.05 s, PURGE commanded at E0 - 2.90 s, theta <= 2 deg, balanced or axial vent, no lining. Needed flow time 1.9 s (<= 1.94 s with the FD-01 element), P_abort 0 at W 0.65 s in 200 000 samples per case, N2O vented 34.3-72.9 g (plan <= 76 g), E0 -> E2 max 0.626 s on the worst purged state, so the 0.81 s plan holds (SIM, `rg01_line_states_v2p4.json`). The claim is gated by CF-4 (founders; `CF-4-SPEC.md`).
4. **Stuck-open bleed (§2.8, §9.1).** 31.24 g/s with both taps liquid (upper bound); additive tank outflow governs (GNC S313). Two NC valves in series: a stuck-open path then needs two faults or one common cause, but the pair doubles the fail-closed probability (no purge, so no relight on that pass). Both go to the FMEA.
5. **Relight latency (RC-03; §7.7).** Latency distributions by line state are delivered to GNC (Table 2.15); the GNC re-fly runs on `rg01_line_states_v2p4.json` (`2939549811d4c381`).
6. **MEOP basis (RC-08; §3.6).** 44.33 bar at the 24 C interlock with the designed line loss and the conservative bands: margin 0.67 bar (1.5 %) to MEOP 45 bar; proof 67.5 bar keeps >= 21.9 bar (SIM).
7. **Trapped volumes, relief and stored energy (RC-04, RC-05, RC-12; §2.7, §9.2, §10.2).** Relief on every lockable volume; stored energy of the flight tank 0.77 kg TNT equivalent by decomposition (6.9 kPa at 16.4 m), ground 15 L tank 4.0 kg (28.4 m) (DERIVED).
8. **Throat life (RC-06; §5.4).** Plan one insert per flight until T1/T4 bore data exist; the literature-kinetics case gives 0.38-0.60 A1 flights on the gnc.json v3 p99 cycle (SIM).
9. **Regression band (RC-07; §4.1, §4.6).** The whole band is run. ENG-06 (web >= 1 mm) holds on A1 and B2 and fails on B1 85 m at a x1.5 with n <= 0.5; "85 m covers 62 m" is withdrawn; B1 flies only under the lead's gating rule after T1-T4.
10. **Minor items.** eta_cf band 0.95-0.975 (RC-09); L* 2.03 m on 300.9 cm3 (RC-16); Bartz curvature stated (RC-17); eroded-throat separation at constant mass flow (RC-18); seat insert TC-03 in Inconel 718 and the coast leak ceiling 0.0055 g/s (RC-02, RC-13 g); cleaning, drying and catch-volume rules (RC-14; §10.1); load-bearing material data still TO-VERIFY (RC-15).
11. **Nozzle joint (§6.5).** The NZ-03 mica/vermiculite gasket (900 C rating) sees 1595 C in Mechanical's FE: it fails by 695 K. Proposal J1: a 2.0 mm carbon-phenolic flange with a 0.5 mm flexible-graphite seal at the lip face. An unsealed joint leaks 0.83-1.10 % of the engine flow and could melt the 316L lip in 1.07-5.70 s (lumped bound); hence hazard H19 and an N2 leak check at 67.5 bar before and after every firing.
