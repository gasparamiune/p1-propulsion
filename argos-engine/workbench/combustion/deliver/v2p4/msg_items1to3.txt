FMEA INPUT FA-1 (single-control hazard; LEAD decision line 160 (4)): fuel-assisted fast part at an A1 relight.
- Hazard: the main N2O reaches the 32.2 mm port of the warm grain with no torch flame, accumulates, and lights late.
  - The fast part (1.2-2.1 g, vapour and spray in transit) burns with fuel vapour.
  - Head Pc: 48.9-75.5 bar abs at a 1 ms burn time. It is above MEOP 45 for <= 3.0 ms and above proof 67.5 at a 60 ms residence time.
  - At a 5 ms burn time <= 55.5 bar; at 10 ms <= 41.4 bar.
  - The rounded entrance does not help at 32.2 mm.
  - SIM on ASSUMED burn time and fuel fraction (late_light_spike_v2p4).
- Single control: the torch-first gate (pintle lift only after E1, torch flame confirmed).
- Failure modes of the control:
  - FM1: false torch confirmation (P_torch bias or drift; photodiode false positive);
  - FM2: lift commanded before E1 (sequence or firmware fault);
  - FM3: torch blown out or quenched by the main flow between E1 and E2;
  - FM4: torch lit but does not ignite the main flow promptly.
- Detection that exists today:
  - Before the start: P_torch auto-zero, and ARM refused above 1.05 bar abs (catches only a gross P_torch bias).
  - At E1: 10 ms persistence.
  - Between E1 and E2: nothing.
  - The 30 bar abort (5 ms raw) and the comparator (RC 5.28 ms) cannot act within a 1-3 ms pulse. LIGHT_SPIKE records the event after the fact; it is evidence, not protection.
- What could detect a gate failure, and its limits:
  - (a) E1 by two dissimilar sensors (P_torch AND photodiode, 2oo2). This cuts FM1 and FM2 (FM2 is also caught by firmware sequence checks in SIL).
  - (b) Continuous torch supervision from E1 to E2 with both sensors; loss for >= 5 ms closes the pintle and MV. This bounds the unlit N2O to about 35-38 g/s x (detection 5 ms + closing 20-100 ms), 0.9-4.0 g.
  - That bound is comparable to the fast part itself, so (b) limits but does NOT remove the hazard (FM3, FM4).
  - Nothing can detect the pulse in time to stop it.
- Severity: catastrophic if the case or closures fail; critical otherwise.
- Occurrence: unknown. No torch reliability data yet; the photodiode false-positive rate is unknown.
- Verification:
  - IGN-1: torch stability under the main N2O flow at the lift (blow-out margin), photodiode false-positive tests, >= 30 lights.
  - T4: relights with LIGHT_SPIKE logging measure the real burn time and peak.

  - Electronics: (a), (b) and the sequence checks.
  - Combustion: refines the bound with IGN-1/T4 data.
  - Mechanical: case and closure response to a 75.5 bar, <= 3 ms pulse (dynamic load factor; burst margin).
  - Lead: acceptance of a single-control hazard.
