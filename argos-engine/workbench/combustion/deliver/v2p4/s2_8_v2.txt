**Question** (LEAD 2026-10-13, Electronics SIL S11 relayed). The vapour between the first liquid and the pintle, in flow order, at RELIGHT governs ignition. In SIL S11 a vapour-full 44 cm3 segment does not light at W 0.65 s on either path. The lead has rejected widening W as the primary fix (CHANGELOG) and will choose between (d) early MV opening under the drogue and (a) a head-end bleed, on this analysis.

**Answer.**
1. **The segment is an inverted U.** The 27.87 cm3 between the U-bottom and the seat cannot reach the MV or S1 by buoyancy in either coast phase. Its crest is 159.6 mm above the U-bottom, and f_z does not reverse after the drogue load in any GNC series.
2. **S1 vents the expansion, not the void.** Once vapour sits at the S1 port, every joule that still boils liquid adds 0.0067 cm3 of vapour to the segment. In every HI heat case the coast-up alone generates more vapour than the segment holds, so **the whole 44 cm3 is vapour at RELIGHT** (the SIL S11 case). In LO the U is 95-100 % vapour and the route holds a cap of 0-8 cm3 behind the first liquid.
3. **W 0.65 s does not light it.** P(abort of a would-be light) is 0.45-0.98 in LO and 1.0 in HI. The 19 cm3 requirement is not met in any heat case.
4. **(d) is refuted for the current routing.** It cannot reach the U, so P_abort at W 0.65 s stays >= 0.45 in every heat case. It also puts tank pressure behind a seated pintle in a hot chamber for 9.3-15.6 s.
5. **(a) is recommended:** a crest bleed with the MV opened first, closing at E0. It lights all six heat cases at W 0.65 s (residual 6.9-9.6 cm3; P_abort 0 in 200 000 samples). It keeps the 0.81 s plan and the 26.6 g inventory basis. Its costs: up to 28.3 g of N2O vented per relight, a new valve and port, and new failure modes (below).
6. **Wider W (rejected by the lead as primary).** A vapour-full segment needs W 1.041 s. Any zero-abort window puts 32.0-41.3 g on a liquid line, against the 26.6 g accepted (prep path W 0.88 s: 32.0-35.1 g). The credible hard-start peak does not depend on that inventory.
7. **The lasting fix is a routing without the U.** It also needs an exchange path to the tank of 12-16 mm with the MV open under the drogue (research case below). Without that path, a no-U routing still fills with vapour in the HI cases.

**Why the U traps vapour.**
- With f_z > 0 (descent), vapour rises toward +z and collects at the crest (z 204.6). The only path from the crest to the MV and S1 runs down the riser and coil to the U-bottom (z 45), then up the wall line. Buoyancy cannot carry vapour 160 mm down, so the U fills from the crest downward. The liquid it displaces leaves through the U-bottom, up the wall line and through S1.
- With f_z < 0 (coast-up), the U-bottom is the high point and both legs leave it toward +z, so nothing leaves the U either.
- The dead-end bore leg (4.51 cm3) holds a liquid plug only if the crest still held liquid when the descent began. Both readings are carried: plug 2.2-2.9 cm3 (LO), or dry.

**Migration along GNC's time histories (RG-20).** `coast_migration_v2p4.json` integrates the drift with the instantaneous seat f_z of the nominal run, of runs 101, 264, 329 and 380 (264 is the minimum-acceleration run), and of the p01/p50/p99 envelope aligned at the relight command. It does not use the shorthand "11.4 s at 8 m/s2".
- **Coast-up** (MECO to the drogue load, 6.8-8.4 s; f_z down to -0.96 to -1.87 m/s2): no motion in the 2-3 mm passages (Eo <= 3.37). The 7.75-8 mm passages move 19-53 mm toward the U-bottom, the filter bore 17-141 mm, and the coil 9-210 mm (Bendiksen bracket). Vapour formed in the coast-up stays essentially where it forms.
- **Dip at the turnover.** The -1.87 / -1.90 m/s2 minimum lies before the drogue load in every series. After the drogue load f_z never reverses: its minimum is 2.47 m/s2 (run 264) and 4.91-4.96 m/s2 in the other series and the envelope. No backward excursion follows the load.
- **Final positive interval** (11.40-13.92 s in the series, 11.67-14.47 s in the envelope). Every leg is traversed with margin before the relight command: wall line 3.1-3.9 s, riser 1.6-2.5 s, drillings 1.0-1.9 s, bore leg 6.7-7.7 s; the smallest margin is 3.76 s (bore leg, run 380). Vapour therefore reaches the crest in every series, and the U fills from the top as stated.
- **GNC drogue window** (`drogue_window_v1.json`, deployment to the relight command): p01 11.80 s at the 0.81 s plan and 10.99-11.80 s across plans, minimum 10.71 s, inflation not modelled. f_z <= 0 lasts at most 0.23-0.27 s at deployment, then rises to 2.45 (p01 1.52) m/s2 at 1 s, 7.34 (6.70) at 3 s and 9.6-9.8 (9.2-9.3) after 6 s; continuous time above 0.5 g is p01 9.60 s. A 0.23 s dip at <= 1.14 m/s2 moves a bubble in the 7.75 mm line by about 6 mm, against the 160 mm the crest would need (DERIVED).
- **Lateral force and tilt.** The specific-force angle from the axis while f_z > 0, after the drogue load, is at most 13.9 deg (run 264; 0-4.4 deg in the other runs). Emptying the U would need the U-bottom held above the crest, i.e. beyond about 71.3 deg with the coil end about 54 mm off the axis (ASSUMED crest on the axis). No series spends any time beyond it. The v2.4-draft wording "angle up to 71 deg" counted the turnover before the drogue load and is withdrawn.

**Filter mesh.** A fully wetted 15 um mesh has a bubble point of about 700 Pa (4 sigma/d; ASSUMED pore = rating). The buoyancy head is 172 Pa across the upper trap and at most 658 Pa across the lower trap, so in the coast the mesh holds vapour below it. That changes where the vapour sits inside the U, not how much there is. In flow the mesh passes gas above 700 Pa of pressure drop, which is negligible at the lift.

**What S1 does and does not do** (`segment_void_balance_v2p4.json`). S1 is a relief: it vents the volume the boiling adds, so the segment stays at P_tank + crack. What it vents depends on what sits at its port.
- While liquid sits at the port, S1 vents liquid. The void then grows by the full vapour volume, 1/(rho_v h_fg) = 0.0388 cm3/J.
- While vapour sits at the port, S1 vents vapour. The void still grows by the volume of liquid evaporated, 1/(rho_l h_fg) = 0.0067 cm3/J. The vapour vented carries away only (v_g - v_l)/h_fg per joule.
- A closed, relief-vented segment therefore never stays liquid-full while it is heated. Only an exchange with the tank (MV open and a path vapour can rise through) or a bleed can return liquid to it.
- **Model.** Over MECO to the relight command, with the heat of each case on the FE Q(t) shape:
  - coast-up: bubbles stay where they form and S1 vents liquid;
  - drogue load: the route's bubbles rise to S1 and form a cap;
  - descent: the U fills from the crest; arrivals at the cap are the route's boiling plus the overflow of a full U; S1 removes only the expansion.
  - After the U is full, its heat either keeps boiling liquid at the U-bottom front ("wet", as VO, heat conducted to the liquid) or stops ("dry", lower bound).
  - Five GNC coast series, six heat cases.

**Option (d): MV opened early under the drogue, pintle seated** (answer to SIL question 1).
- **U exchange: none, at any time.** Every buoyant path from the crest to the MV first descends 159.6 mm to the U-bottom, and f_z stays positive after the drogue load. The U keeps its 26.5-27.9 cm3 whatever the window.
- **Route exchange** through the MV path: Wallis counter-current flooding, 8 mm module drilling ASSUMED, C 0.725-1.0. Capacity is 1.2-4.3 cm3/s of vapour exchanged for liquid. With the MV opened at the drogue load or about 2 s earlier (exposure 9.3-15.6 s in the coast series; GNC's window from deployment p01 10.99-11.80 s, minimum 10.71 s):
  - the cap clears 0.9-3.2 s after opening in the LO cases;
  - in HI it clears in 6.3-11.7 s only if the full U stops boiling;
  - otherwise it never clears: the U's overflow (10.8-19.5 cm3/s at relight) exceeds the path's capacity.
- **Soak-back against clearing:** at relight the U regenerates vapour at 1.9 (isothermal LO) to 19.5 (ARM HI) cm3/s, and the route at 0.39-4.64 cm3/s. The U never clears; the route clears only where its capacity exceeds its generation.
- **Effect on ignition:** P_abort at W 0.65 s stays 0.45-0.54 in every LO case and 0.54-1.0 in HI. (d) removes only the cap behind the first liquid.
- **Risk with the MV open and the pintle seated for 9.3-15.6 s:**
  - Seat leak into the hot chamber: within the B-PNT-1 acceptance (1.0-1.4e-3 g/s liquid N2O) it adds <= 0.022 g over 15.6 s, and the fault-case leaks of §3.5 (13.5-82 mg/s) add 0.2-1.3 g. The closed MV would not have stopped either: the 35.7 g in the segment feeds them anyway.
  - The difference is a stuck-open or drifting pintle. With the MV closed it can deliver at most the segment's 35.7 g. With the MV open the tank (0.825 kg of liquid at relight on A1) feeds it. That would mean a sustained, unplanned flow into a chamber whose grain surface and throat are still hot, i.e. a delayed light or an uncontrolled burn under the drogue. A pyrolysis-premixed delayed light is the case whose 1 ms peak exceeds MEOP (§7.6).
- **Verdict:** (d) is refuted for the inverted-U routing: no relight benefit, and a new hazard.

**Option (a): crest prime bleed** (answer to SIL question 2; `vapour_options_v2p4.json`).

- **New failure modes and controls:**
  - Bleed fails closed: the relight sees the full pocket and aborts. The valve's current and position feedback detects it before E0; the lead decides the response (fallback below).
  - Bleed fails open: 45-76 g/s of liquid overboard, 18-29 % of full-thrust oxidiser flow. The ECU detects it, closes the MV and aborts, or the lead accepts a degraded burn. A second NC valve in series removes the single point (Mechanical and Electronics, mass and cost TO-QUOTE, Sønderborg DK-6400).
  - Jet reaction <= 8 N while liquid flows (<= mdot sqrt(2 dP/rho_l) = 0.0755 kg/s x 107 m/s; DERIVED upper bound): vent aft-facing or balanced (tee) so that no side force reaches the TVC.
  - MV open on the seated pintle for about 1.0-1.3 s instead of 0.35 s: the stuck-open exposure of (d), reduced about tenfold.
  - Port feasibility: a radial fitting at the bonnet leaves the 8 deg sweep cone (Mechanical); the valve must be oxygen-clean and rated MAWP >= 8.42 MPa.

- **The first part is vapour.** The vapour part passes through the chamber at 8.9-13.0 g/s during the 0.64 s purge and mostly leaves through the throat. At any instant 0.85-0.94 g of it is resident (isenthalpic throttling to the 1.07-1.08 bar cold-flow chamber pressure, 300.9 cm3; CoolProp). A liquid start holds 0.95 g of flashed vapour. The fast part of a hard start is therefore no larger with a vapour-first inventory.
- **Hard-start peak against MEOP 45 bar** (credible case, flight, no disc; the same inputs as §7.6; every inventory from 6.9 to 40.3 g):
  - tf 1 ms: 43.7-44.9 bar at the A1 state with a sharp entrance; 40.1-41.5 bar with the rounded entrance; <= 42.4 bar at 20 C and <= 40.3 bar at 25 C;
  - tf 5 ms: <= 29.2 bar.
  - The peak does not depend on the inventory: the fast part sets it. The margin to MEOP is 0.1 bar (sharp) and 3.5 bar (rounded); the rounded entrance is required on every grain (§7.6).
- What changes with the inventory is the duration of the event and the mass that can react if the fast-part estimate is wrong. Every input of the credible case is ASSUMED, so an acceptance of 32-41 g rests on the same unmeasured basis as the 26.6 g.
- Under the "restart" reading the ARM LO states (U full, short slug, cap 6-8 cm3) still abort 18-25 % at W 1.05 s.

**The lasting fix: a routing without the U** (research case; `segment_void_balance_v2p4.json` `noU_*`).
- Hypothesis: with the line rising monotonically from the seat to the MV, every vapour except the 4.51 cm3 bore-leg annulus collects at the top, behind the liquid, and the relight lights on that liquid. With the MV open under the drogue, the top can exchange with the tank.
- Results (W 0.65 s):
  - **No U, S1 only:** the LO cases light (P_abort 0). Their cap of 7-31 cm3 sits behind a 8-32 cm3 liquid slug. ARM LO still aborts 11-54 % under the restart reading. **Every HI case is vapour-full** and aborts.
  - **No U plus the MV opened at the drogue load:** with an 8 mm exchange path, LO and the dry-HI reading light, but the wet HI cases stay vapour-full. With 12 mm the isothermal HI case lights (cap 18-24 cm3 behind a 16-22 cm3 slug); hot wall +5 K HI aborts 43-64 % and ARM HI stays vapour-full. **With 16 mm every case lights.**
- Comparator: (a) on the present routing.
- Evidence needed: Mechanical's routing with its exchange path diameter (MV, module, line), and its heat cases on that routing.
- Refutation criterion: the re-routed heat cases leave the liquid slug at RELIGHT shorter than 14 cm3 (0.33 s of liquid flow, the ASSUMED ignition delay plus confirmation), or the exchange path cannot reach 12-16 mm within the MV and module.
- A U of <= 18.9 cm3 alone does not meet the requirement. In the HI cases the coast-up fills the whole segment through S1 whatever the U volume.

**Recommendation (Combustion; the lead decides).** **Adopt (a)**, the crest bleed sized for 44 cm3 (1.56 mm, 0.375 s; or 1.20 mm, 0.625 s), closing at E0 (no earlier than 0.5 s before it), with W kept at 0.65 s and the 0.81 s plan from T_fire.
- Evidence:
  - (a) is the only option that removes the vapour before the lift: P_abort 0 in all six heat cases at W 0.65 s (residual 6.9-9.6 cm3);
  - (d) cannot reach the U (P_abort >= 0.45 in every case) and adds a tank-fed stuck-pintle hazard;
  - a wider W needs 1.041 s for the vapour-full HI states, and its zero-abort windows put 32-41 g on a liquid line.
- Costs for the lead: <= 28.3 g of N2O per relight (GNC margin); a valve, a port, a harness channel and the PRE-BLEED step (Mechanical, Electronics); a fail-open mode needing either a series valve or an abort rule.
- **Fallback if the bleed is detected failed before E0:** the lead chooses between no relight (vehicle lost under the drogue) and the prep path at W 0.88 s with up to 35.1 g on a liquid line (34.8 g at 24 C). No other fallback lights the HI states.
- **Refutation criteria for (a):**
  - CF-4 with a heated U (vapour-full, then bled): the first sustained liquid at the injector arrives > 0.29 s after the lift (18.9 cm3 at 0.0156 s/cm3), or the bleed needs more than 0.30 s at 1.56 mm to pass liquid at the crest port;
  - IGN-1/T1: the ignition delay p99.9 exceeds 0.30 s, which moves every allowance above;
  - Mechanical's heat cases put the U's regeneration after closure above 19.5 cm3/s (ARM HI), which would shorten the allowed gap below 0.5 s;
  - GNC: P(landing) with 28.3 g less N2O per relight falls below the lead's threshold.

**Research proposal (not adopted): sensed bleed closure.**
- Hypothesis: a thermocouple just downstream of the bleed orifice sees liquid arrival as a step toward the 1 bar saturation temperature (-88.5 C), because flashing liquid cools the jet far more than choked vapour. Closing on that signal would cut the vented N2O from 28.3 g to about 6-12 g on a primed line.
- Comparator: the fixed 0.375 s bleed.
- Evidence needed: a CF-4 run with a 0.5 mm sheathed thermocouple in the bleed jet.
- Refutation criterion: the signal takes longer than 50 ms, or does not separate vapour from liquid.

**Research proposal (not adopted): abort deadline referenced to liquid arrival.**
- Hypothesis: the chamber pressure at the ignition lift separates vapour flow from liquid flow. Cold liquid flow gives 0.15-0.25 of the lit Pc; vapour gives less, about 28 % of the liquid mass flow. Liquid arrival is then detectable within about 20 ms.
- Rule: abort at (liquid arrival + 0.65 s), capped at 1.05 s from the lift. That bounds the unignited liquid at the W 0.65 s level whatever the vapour pocket.
- Comparator: a fixed W.
- Evidence needed: CF-4 traces of Pc and P_feed at the lift with a heated U.
- Refutation criterion: the signatures overlap, or detection takes longer than 50 ms.

**What the PRIMED flag does not see.** A segment full of saturated vapour reads P_feed = P_tank, so it reads PRIMED; MSS-1 and MSS-2 read T_sat either way (Electronics, REV-GNC-1 answers). The flag decides only the fault path. Under (a) every relight bleeds, whatever the flag says. Mechanical's seat-leak drain analysis shows that unpriming the segment within a coast needs >= 1 g/s, i.e. a stuck-open pintle; UNPRIMED in flight is a fault indication.
