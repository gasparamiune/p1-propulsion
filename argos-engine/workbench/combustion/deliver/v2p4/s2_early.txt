**v2.4 note (sequence v2.8 and the A1 purge).** On an A1 relight the liquid must cover the tank outlet from the PURGE command, E0 - 2.90 s, not only from T_fire: the purge is sized for liquid at both taps (15.62 g/s per tap) and vents vapour at 4.25 g/s per tap. The settling condition above therefore applies from E0 - 2.90 s - t_settle. Under the drogue the axial specific force at RELIGHT is 0.94-1.14 g (GNC records), so the condition is expected to hold; GNC confirms it on the re-fly (§12). If the outlet uncovers during PURGE, the vented mass falls and the segment may not clear: the relight then rests on the line-state tables of §2.8 for an incomplete purge, which the T_fire delay rule (LEAD line 164) does not cover. That case is listed in §11.
