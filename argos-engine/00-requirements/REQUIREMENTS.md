## 1. Mission the engine must enable

The design case is the baseline flight **A1** at ACEMA, Argentina, 1 Sept 2027 (LEAD, 2026-10-12, OI-1 decision):
1. Vertical liftoff under TVC.
2. Full-thrust ascent.
3. Pre-MECO throttle-down to ≤ 75 %, then MECO.
4. Coast nose-first, stabilised by 4 aft fins, to a **300 m** apogee.
5. A nose drogue sets the engine-down descent attitude.
6. In-flight relight, with the NC main valve closed during the coast.
7. Throttled, gimballed powered descent with drogue release.
8. Upright touchdown at ≤ 2 m/s vertical and ≤ 0.5 m/s lateral.

Development flights (LEAD, 2026-10-12):
- **B1**: continuous-burn hop, capped at **62 m provisional** (was 85 m; amended 2026-10-13). The cap is a target, not an apex bound: size the safety area on the apex distribution (p99 about 69-70 m, REV-GNC-1 RG-10). Fly only after the cap is re-derived on the measured engine (T1-T4) and the 1 mm-web burn limit is implemented.
- **B2**: continuous-burn hop to 50 m.
- **C**: tethered hover.
- **D**: static-fire qualification.
- **A2** (finless free-fall, 300 m) is removed from the 2.00 kg plan. In dispersed flights it tumbles in coast and peaks at 213–266 m. If A2 is ever wanted, it needs a 2.20 kg / 3.49 L tank.

## 3. Key component baselines (LEAD, with details in the interface files)

- **Pintle injector:**
  - Geometry: 5.5 mm seat, 15° needle, 4 mm stroke, 3.0 mm stem; 17-4PH needle (H900) and seat (H1150); lapped land with blued contact ≥ 0.24 mm and a PFPE film.
  - Seal: spring-energised PTFE rod seal (vendor TO-VERIFY).
  - Drive: MKS HV69 servo in-line, toggle crank on its own bearings, one-sided dog coupling.
  - Guide and spring: **rolling-guide** carriage, spiral spring 0.25 N·m + 0.12 N·m/rad.
  - Force-law rule: closing assist is checked on L0; opening on L1 and L2, with opening margin ≥ 1.5.
- **TVC actuators:** two SB-2292SG servos through a Cardan crank linkage at n = 4.8, on a 2S supply direct; configurable per-axis notch.
- **Feed:**
  - NC isolation valve Cv ≥ 3, mounted off the gimbal.
  - Filter ≤ 15 µm at Cv ≥ 2.2.
  - Priming: 0.10 mm pintle bleed lift before the valve opens, then a valve ramp ≥ 0.3 s.
- **Ignition:** spark-ignited N2O/propane torch, at least 3 starts.
