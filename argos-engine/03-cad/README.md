# ARGOS hopper engine - CAD v3.4.1 (design model)

Mechanical, 2026-10-13.  Generated by `03-cad/src/make_all.py`; do not edit outputs by hand.

## Status

Design model of the gimballed N2O hybrid engine, its gimbal, TVC linkage, feed (riser TEE, O3b hose coil, fixed 3/8 line) and the
MV-P main-valve module.  It is **not released for fabrication**: drawings are design-model projections with the fits and notes the
parts carry; tolerances, weld procedures and inspection plans are open.  Geometric checks (validity, interference, clearance, sweep,
STEP round-trip, GLB read-back) are software checks; they are not physical validation.

## Frames and units

- CAD / STEP: millimetres, engine frame, +Z forward (nozzle to injector), origin at the gimbal pivot at neutral, +X outer gimbal axis,
  +Y inner gimbal axis.  Parts modelled in place carry engine-frame coordinates in their STEP files.
- GLB: metres, +Y up, (x, y, z)_glTF = (x, z, -y)_engine / 1000.  Visualisation only (tessellated).

## Contents

- `parts/` - 98 part numbers: STEP (verified round-trip) and STL.
- `assembly/` - ENGINE_v3.4.1_neutral.step (every instance, re-import verified), ENGINE_v3.4.1_stop045.step (8 deg stop,
  visual), ENGINE_v3.4.1_neutral.glb, assembly_manifest_v3.4.1.json.
- `drawings/` - design-model drawings (PDF, SVG, DXF) of the 51 machined parts.
- `BOM_v3.4.1.csv/.json` - one line per part number; every price TO-QUOTE (delivery Sonderborg, Denmark, DK-6400) unless dated.
- `massprops_v3.4.1.json` - groups, burn states, CG and inertia about the CG and the pivot.
- `checks/` - validity, interference and clearance at 17 poses, bay-wall clearance, swept envelope, round-trips, GLB read-back.
- `src/` - the generator (parameters from the interface files through config.py; calcs/ holds the design calculations).
